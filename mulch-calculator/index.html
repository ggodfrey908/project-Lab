How to calculate mulch volume

Measure the garden bed length and width in feet and choose a depth in inches. Multiply length by width by depth in feet, then divide the cubic feet by 27 to convert to cubic yards.

Example estimate

A 20 by 6 foot bed covered with 3 inches of mulch needs 30 cubic feet, or approximately 1.11 cubic yards before any extra allowance.

Before you buy materials

Avoid piling mulch against tree trunks or plant stems. Appropriate depth depends on your mulch type, soil and plants. All results are planning estimates, not professional construction advice.
