Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ProjectLab handles information when you visit our free DIY calculator website.

Information you enter into calculators

Our paint, flooring, mulch and concrete calculators perform calculations in your browser. We do not ask you to create an account, and the measurements you enter are not intentionally sent to ProjectLab or saved in a ProjectLab database. If you use the Copy estimate feature, your browser copies the estimate to your clipboard at your request.

Hosting and technical information

ProjectLab is hosted using Cloudflare Pages. When you visit the website, Cloudflare may process technical information such as your IP address, browser and device information, request details and security-related information to deliver and protect the site. Cloudflare handles that information under its own policies. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Analytics and advertising

ProjectLab has not enabled a separate website analytics service such as Google Analytics or Cloudflare Web Analytics and does not currently display third-party advertisements. We have not intentionally added advertising or analytics tracking code to the site. Hosting and security services may still process technical information as described above.

If we introduce analytics or advertising in the future, we will update this policy before enabling those services and provide any notices or consent controls required by applicable law.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your message and manage the correspondence. Please do not send sensitive personal information or payment details. Your email provider and ours may also process email data under their own policies.

External links

ProjectLab may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as ProjectLab changes. The latest version and its update date will appear on this page.
