How to estimate concrete for a slab

Multiply slab length by width in feet, then multiply by thickness converted from inches to feet. Divide the volume by the yield listed on your concrete bag, allowing for extra material.

Example estimate

A 10 by 8 foot slab that is 4 inches thick has about 26.67 cubic feet of concrete, or 0.99 cubic yards. With a 10% allowance and 0.6 cubic feet per 80 lb bag, the estimate is 49 bags.

Before you buy materials

Bag yield varies by brand and mixing conditions. For load-bearing or structural slabs, consult a qualified professional. All results are planning estimates, not professional construction advice.
